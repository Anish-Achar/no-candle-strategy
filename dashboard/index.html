Read these numbers with care

  • Each pair was tuned and measured on the same ten months of data. Settings found this way usually do worse on new data.
  • No commission and no spread. TradingView's OANDA prices don't charge the spread, so settings with many trades look better here than they would live.
  • Larger position sizes grow profit and drawdown together. A size that stays under $10,000 drawdown in this period can still exceed it later.
  • Leverage multiplies costs as well as profits. No spread or commission is included; with typical spreads of about 1–2.5 pips, 50× (500% of equity per trade) would cost very roughly 5–20% of the account over these trades. Many regulators also cap retail forex leverage (for example 30:1 on major pairs in the UK and EU), so 50× may not be available to you.
  • TradingView shows a "may use look-ahead bias" caution on this strategy. The script is written to avoid look-ahead, but check a few fills on the chart before trusting the numbers.
  • Ranges tested: trend mode (all 4), pivot 1–5, EMA 50–300, validity 3–20, pending 1–5, wick tolerance 0–10 ticks, swing stop buffer 0–20 ticks with or without the wick, NO-range stop 0.5–6×, reward:risk 0.5–3, direction, position size 5–100% of equity, and leverage of 10×, 20× and 50× (10% size × leverage).
